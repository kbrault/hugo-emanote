Internal links (to other pages on this site) are highlighted in color and take you straight to that page when clicked, like this one to [Writing Your First Note](/guide/first-note/). They're easy to spot in the text.

External links (to other websites), like [the Hugo docs](https://gohugo.io/documentation/), stay plain text, with an underline and a small arrow that shows up on hover. 

Mail links get their own little icon too: [email me](mailto:hello@example.com).
